About Us

Future Leaders Initiative Zambia is a nonprofit organization dedicated to empowering rural youth through education, digital literacy, leadership development, and entrepreneurship awareness.

Our Programs

Digital Literacy

Teaching computer basics, internet safety, and responsible technology use.

Leadership Development

Building confidence, communication, teamwork, and critical thinking skills.

Entrepreneurship

Introducing business thinking, innovation, and problem-solving skills.

School Outreach

Working directly with rural schools, teachers, and communities.

Mentorship

Connecting youth with mentors, professionals, and role models.

Our Impact

  • Improving access to education in rural areas
  • Increasing digital literacy among youth
  • Strengthening leadership skills
  • Encouraging entrepreneurship mindset
  • Building future-ready communities
